Upload artwork, pick a height, get a print-ready PDF of foldable D&D paper miniatures.

Drop images here or click to browse — PNG, JPG or WebP. Drop several at once to add a row each.
Image Height Copies

A height slot sets how tall a figure prints, and its width follows the artwork — except very wide artwork, which is scaled down whole and prints a little short. Normalize artwork trims transparent or flat-colour margins. Details close to the background colour may also be trimmed. Turn it off to print the original artwork. The slot also carries a size category, which sets the base width. A ! badge means the artwork could not be trimmed or the mini is too large.
